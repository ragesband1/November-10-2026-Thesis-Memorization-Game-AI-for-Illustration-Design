Part 1: Core Definitions & Variables

Your study examines 9 key variables affecting the behavioral intention of 30 LPU Multimedia Arts students to use AI for illustration and design.

Key AI tools evaluated: ChatGPT for brainstorming and text prompts, Google Gemini for creative reasoning and text-to-image, Adobe Firefly for commercial-safe professional design and generative fill, and Canva AI for final layout creation and posters.

Core Finding: AI dramatically improves efficiency and productivity, but carries risks like overdependence and loss of personal originality. Furthermore, 24 variables show a statistically significant relationship while 5 variables show no significant relationship.

Part 2: Survey Questionnaire & Themes

Respondent demographics include Student Number 2021-1-02203, Course AB Multimedia Arts. The instrument uses a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree) across 30 statements mapping to 9 major themes.

Part 3: Frameworks & Recommendations

Theoretical underpinning relies heavily on the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) focusing on perceived usefulness and ease of use.

Ultimate Conclusion: Human creativity and critical thinking remain paramount; AI serves as a complementary design assistant rather than a replacement for freehand and traditional artistic skills. Recommendations emphasize ethics, transparency, and personal originality.
